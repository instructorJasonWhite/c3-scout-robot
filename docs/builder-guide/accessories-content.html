Storage cases · all three variants are v8

A case for each Scout configuration

Choose the case that matches the front of your assembled Scout: standard bumper, angled OLED bumper or upright ESP32-CAM mount. Each has a captive hinge and a separate flat-printed spring latch.

Version 8 keeps the broad shell at a nominal 2.8 mm, with 5 mm bevels, taller side guides and rounded rear locators. Each download includes editable Fusion and STEP files, complete and separate STL meshes, a Bambu Studio project, images and print notes.

Shared print setup

Print the captive pair and its matching latch

Start with the supplied v8 Bambu Studio project. Its orientation keeps the base and lid in their intended hinge relationship and lays the separate spring on its broad face. Keep that relationship when adapting the project to another printer.

SettingVerified P1S setup
Printer and materialBambu P1S · 0.4 mm nozzle · PLA Basic, filament 1 · textured PEI
Layers and walls0.20 mm layers · 4 walls · 5 top layers · 3 bottom layers
Infill15% grid
Supports and adhesion extrasNo supports, brim or prime tower in the supplied setup

Use the complete STL or the separate -Box.stl and -Latch.stl files. Loading both sets duplicates the parts. Never independently arrange the captive base and lid. Older v6/v7 sliced plates do not apply to v8.

  1. Check the selected printer, plate, material and layer preview before printing. Use the profile as a starting point and confirm fit with your printer and material.
  2. Let the print cool, then gently free the captive hinges.
  3. With the lid open, align the latch’s thick dovetail foot with the internal entry channel and slide it toward the lid roof until seated. Hold the foot rather than levering the flexible blade.
  4. Close gently for the first test. To open, push the catch inward through the front opening, lift the lid through the first few degrees, then release the catch.

Print times are slicer estimates. The complete cases still need a physical fit check. Nominal 2.8 mm walls can be thinner at shallow decorative recesses.

Optional front bumpers · v1 prototypes

Add an OLED or a camera mount

These replacement bumpers reuse Scout’s two original M3 bumper screws and 56 mm mounting-hole spacing. Choose the matching storage case above when keeping the accessory attached.

Angled OLED bumper

Designed for the 0.96-inch DIYmall B01HEBIJKK board, 27.3 mm square with a 20.7 × 23.2 mm mounting pattern. Add four M2 × 12 screws and nuts. Current Scout firmware supports the display; see OLED wiring and faces.

OLED assembly guide · OLED bumper package

ESP32-CAM mount

The two-piece mount adjusts for a 10–25 mm stack depth, with 28 mm inside width and a 41 mm camera seat height. Add two M3 × 12 screws, two nuts and four washers. Check your actual kit’s dimensions and cable access.

Camera assembly guide · Camera mount package

Both bumper mounts are prototypes awaiting physical fit checks. CAD and mesh checks passed. Camera firmware, streaming and integration with Scout’s power or controller are separate work. The camera needs a suitable regulated supply through its adapter; do not power it from a C3 GPIO. Follow the detailed guides before assembly.
